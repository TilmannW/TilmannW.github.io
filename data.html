Talkshow Dataset of Germany (TDOG)

A comprehensive dataset on Germany's political talk-show universe, 1998–2025.

TDOG provides episode- and guest-level information on Germany's major national political talk shows. It covers more than 6,000 episodes and over 7,300 hours of airtime, combining broadcast dates and program information with guest identities, political affiliations, and detailed topic classifications.

The dataset is designed to study media gatekeeping, political visibility, agenda setting, and the effects of televised political exposure. It is used in my project “Talk Shows, Populism, and the Far Right”.
