About

Photo

I am a PhD candidate in Political Economy at Heidelberg University, where I work at the Chair of International and Development Politics of Axel Dreher.

My research interests lie at the intersection of political economy, development economics, and applied microeconomics. In my current research, I study political communication and public opinion, international organizations and political polarization, migration, and the historical origins of culture.

Before joining Heidelberg University, I studied International Political Economy at the University of Marburg and the University of Texas at Dallas, as well as International and Development Economics at HTW Berlin.
