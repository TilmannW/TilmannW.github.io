I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at Ruprecht Karl University of Heidelberg, supervised by Prof. Dr. Axel Dreher at the Chair of International and Development Politics.

My research interests are political economy, development economics, economic history, and migration. I study how media exposure, electoral incentives, international economic policies, and development finance shape political behavior and the allocation of resources.

Before starting my Ph.D., I studied International Political Economy at Philipps University of Marburg and the University of Texas at Dallas, International and Development Economics at HTW Berlin, and Socioeconomics at the University of Hamburg, including a semester at Stellenbosch University. In 2025, I was a visiting Ph.D. student at the University of Hong Kong.
